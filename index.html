About

I'm a self-driven learner. When my diploma program did not include an attachment, I chose to build a real system instead — Universal Information Management System (UIMS), a multi-tenant platform for institutions that need to manage people, cameras, and compliance in one place. Building it taught me what coursework does not: how production PostgreSQL Row-Level Security works, why datetime handling between systems is harder than it looks, and how to ship a feature end-to-end.

I care about systems that are correct before they are clever. Invoice numbers that cannot skip. Audit logs that record who changed what. Datetimes that mean the same instant on both sides of an API. These are small things individually, and they are the difference between a demo and something a business can rely on.
